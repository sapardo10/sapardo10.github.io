Privacy policy

Effective November 3, 2026. This policy describes the Caurora iPhone camera app, version 1.0.

Summary

Caurora has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking. The developer does not collect personal information through Caurora. Location is optional and approximate; it is used on your iPhone and sent only to Apple Weather for cloud cover.

Your photographs

Caurora uses camera access only to provide the viewfinder and capture photographs, including combining several frames into one photo on your device. Photo access is add-only: Caurora can save new photos to your library, with your permission, but cannot read your existing photos. Caurora does not upload photos to the developer, NOAA or anyone else. Your own Photos and iCloud settings may sync them through Apple.

Local settings and unsaved captures

Camera preferences, such as exposure, self-timer and the focus position saved for each lens, are stored on your device. The last forecast response is cached on your device so the app can show how old it is. If a save fails, the photo stays temporarily in app memory so you can retry. It is removed from app memory when saved, discarded, or the app exits. Temporary RAW files used for a save attempt are removed afterward.

Location (optional)

Caurora asks for location only when you tap Tonight, and only as approximate location. It is used on your iPhone to work out the aurora outlook near you and whether the sky is dark enough. To show cloud cover, Caurora sends your approximate location to Apple's WeatherKit service; Apple states that this location is not associated with personally identifiable information and is not tracked between requests. Apple privacy policy. Your location is never sent to NOAA or to the developer, and Caurora does not store a location history. If you decline, Tonight shows global activity only and the camera works normally.

NOAA forecast requests

Caurora downloads public aurora and geomagnetic forecasts from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center. The whole forecast is downloaded and looked up on your device, so the request does not include photographs, camera settings, identifiers, or your location. NOAA and network providers receive standard connection information such as the IP address needed to deliver the response. Their own privacy policies govern their systems. NOAA privacy information. Caurora is not affiliated with NOAA.

Motion and permissions

Device motion is used locally to display a horizon level. Caurora does not store or transmit motion data. Camera, location, motion and photo-library permissions can be changed at any time in iPhone Settings.

No tracking or accounts

Caurora has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated backend. The developer does not collect personal information through Caurora.

Control and contact

Delete Caurora to remove its local settings and cached forecast; already-saved photos remain in Photos until you delete them there. For support or privacy questions, contact carnifis@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy will be updated if Caurora's data practices change.
