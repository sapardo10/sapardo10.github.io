Stampway ("the Application") is a mobile application that allows users to create, manage, and store digital travel stamps. We are committed to protecting your personal data and ensuring transparency regarding how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded in accordance with applicable laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

We may collect the following categories of personal data:
